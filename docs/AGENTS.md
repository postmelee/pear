# Official documentation rules

Read the [root rules](../AGENTS.md), [documentation index](README.md), relevant requirements and the approved task's document-location judgment before changes.

- Keep product, user, contributor and technical guidance in this official `docs/` root. Keep Issue plans, approvals, stage/final reports and workflow manuals in `mydocs/`.
- Read existing content before editing, preserve unrelated material and update affected links. Add or relocate documents only within the approved location judgment.
- Maintain [delivery-plan.md](delivery-plan.md) as the single roadmap. README, Wiki and community discussions point to adopted requirements instead of maintaining competing copies. Unregistered candidates must remain visibly unregistered.
- Distinguish normative requirements, dated research/observations, live GitHub work state and verified qualification. Technical M1, the ordinary-user outcome and independent R1 have different acceptance scopes.
- Cite primary sources with exact revision/version and review date where they affect a decision. Source inspection, installed-tool availability and actual execution are separate results. Record conflicting guidance and unresolved limitations rather than copying examples as guarantees.
- Keep contribution guidance usable without Codex, paid AI or mandatory agent tooling. Preserve upstream license and contribution-policy boundaries.
- Follow [Apple tooling guidance](development/apple-tooling.md) only for relevant technical work; its developer-tool prerequisites do not establish Pear's minimum end-user OS or qualified runtime support.

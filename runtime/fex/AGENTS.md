# FEX component rules

Read the [root rules](../../AGENTS.md), [runtime rules](../AGENTS.md), approved Task #5 plans and current stage report before changes.

- Component records are experimental source/build observations with `scope: fex_component`. They are not additional product contracts or runtime/game/distribution eligibility.
- Keep the pinned FEX tree, selected gitlink mounts, applicable notices and original #1 source decision intact. Retain upstream C++/assembly. No AI-generated code is submitted to FEX upstream; its contribution policy is separate from downstream MIT reuse.
- Preserve #4's 23-patch baseline and materialized-source identity. Future CPU supplements have independent ordered identities; coordinate shared Wine helpers and graphics-layer ordering with the root owner.
- Prepare only fresh owned `.build/task5/` outputs from verified read-only caches. Source scripts, submodule updates and recorded commands are never executed by preparation. Preserve original failures and caches.
- Stage 1 includes source/tool/static-link/interface closure only. Full custom CRT/module construction, Darwin service execution, JIT ownership, translator initialization and guest dispatch require their later approved stages.
- Preserve explicit unsupported/unavailable/unperformed observations. Keep disk cache and profiling disabled and software TSO enabled; no unsupported inherited override or fake service success.
- The concrete executable-memory design requires requester review before a prototype. Preserve normal macOS protections and actual per-executable authorization. No new deliberate native fatal-fault fixture or retry of the blocked no-TEB route.
- Keep proprietary content, credentials, prefixes, saves, profiles and raw private diagnostics out of Git. Export only reviewed allowlisted derivative evidence.
